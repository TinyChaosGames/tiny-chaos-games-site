1. About this policy

This policy applies to ONE MORE PIXEL for Android. Tiny Chaos Games is the publisher's brand, not a separate legal entity. The Game does not require an account or operate a Tiny Chaos Games server storing player accounts, scores, or gameplay progress. Certain Google services are integrated as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores scores and records, game and challenge progress, earned Pixel Shards, unlocked and selected cosmetics, settings, locally cached supporter entitlements, advertising-frequency counters, a local age category and review time where applicable, and information relating to privacy choices through Google's tools. The Game does not ask you to create a profile or provide your name, email address, phone number or postal address. Local gameplay records are not uploaded to a Tiny Chaos Games game-data server.

3. Age check

The Game uses a neutral age check to classify users as child (under 13), teen (13–17), adult (18+), or unknown. A date of birth is used to determine the category but the full birth date is not stored persistently by the Game. The category and, when applicable, a recheck time may be stored locally. The age check is not advertising consent. Applicable legal age requirements vary by location.

4. Children and teens

Children, teens, and users with an unknown age category can use core gameplay, challenges, earned rewards, and free cosmetics. In the audited build023 these categories do not initialize Google Mobile Ads, request ads, request consent through UMP, initialize the Google Play Billing purchase connection, receive purchase offers, or see in-game commercial links. The Google libraries are still packaged with the Android app; these restrictions describe the audited runtime behavior rather than guaranteeing no processing by platform components.

5. Google advertising and consent tools

The audited app contains Google Mobile Ads SDK 24.9.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 3.2.0. Adult users may be presented with privacy choices through UMP when required. Ad services are subject to age and privacy controls. Production ads are disabled in the audited build023; they may only be enabled in a future updated release. When Google's services are active, Google may process information such as IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), advertising and app interactions, diagnostics, and app/device identifiers depending on the configuration. The audited manifest does not request the Android AD_ID or device-location permissions, but this does not establish that no technical identifiers or personal data are processed. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

6. Optional supporter purchases

The Game includes Google Play Billing 9.1.0 for optional one-time supporter purchases made available to eligible adult users. Google Play processes payments. The Game may receive product identifiers, transaction status and purchase tokens needed to check and acknowledge purchases; it does not receive payment card details. In the audited implementation raw purchase tokens are not deliberately stored or logged; purchase entitlement status may be cached locally. The publisher does not currently operate a backend purchase-verification service. Google's handling of purchase information is governed by its own policies.

7. Purposes and legal grounds

Local game settings, progress and age classifications are used to provide gameplay, keep earned items and progress, and apply age-based safeguards. Where personal data processing is necessary for these functions, the applicable legal basis may include performance of the service requested by the user or legitimate interests in safe and functional operation, subject to applicable law and balancing requirements. For adult users, Google services may support privacy choices, optional purchases, and, only if enabled in a later release, advertising. The legal grounds for Google's own processing and any required consent depend on the applicable service and jurisdiction; Google's consent and privacy notices provide additional information. An age classification alone is not consent to advertising.

8. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until you change it, reset it using available Game functions, clear app storage, or uninstall the Game. Android backup is disabled in the audited build023 manifest, subject to verification in the final release. The publisher does not keep server-side copies of local gameplay progress. Clearing or uninstalling the Game does not necessarily erase information held independently by Google, such as purchase records or consent-related data. For privacy requests, contact the publisher using the details above.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and, where applicable, data portability, as well as the right to withdraw consent without affecting processing previously based on it. You may complain to the competent data protection authority, including the Polish President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), where appropriate. Contact games.tinychaos@gmail.com regarding data handled by the publisher. For data independently handled by Google, use Google's privacy controls and contact channels.

10. Security and international transfers

The Game seeks to minimize information stored locally. Google services may process data outside your country subject to their published legal safeguards. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted data in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Privacy options and updates

Eligible adult users may be able to manage privacy choices through UMP. Opening this policy and managing advertising choices are separate functions. Declining advertising choices does not block core gameplay. This policy may change if the Game's implementation, SDKs, or applicable rules change; the effective date above will be updated.
